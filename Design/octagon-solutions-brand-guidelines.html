01 — Brand essence

The form between order and the market.

The octagon mediates between the square — order, rules, the institutions of the State — and the circle — continuity, movement, the market. That is exactly where Octagon operates: between the company and the government, translating one into the other.

The bridge

Eight sides, eight points of contact with the public sector. We sit at the seam where private enterprise meets public power.

Eight — prosperity

Eight is the number of abundance and of open doors. A firm that opens the way in every direction.

Mission

Enable companies to sell to the Brazilian public sector — turning regulatory complexity into real market access, with legal certainty.

Vision

A Brazil where the best technology reaches the public sector. Where the State buys from those who build the future.

02 — The mark

One illuminated wedge.

The logo is a black octagon with a single highlighted wedge — the octant, the way in — split diagonally into gold and grey: the moment enterprise meets the State.

03 — Logo misuse

Protect the mark.

✓

Use the mark in full gold-and-grey on black, paper, or white.

✕

Don't recolour the wedge or swap gold for another hue.

✕

Don't stretch, skew, or change the octagon's proportions.

✕

Don't rotate the mark — the wedge always sits top-right.

✕

Don't add shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines to the mark.

✕

Don't place the mark on a busy photo or low-contrast field.

Surface rule. Gold is surface-dependent. It sings on black and quiets on light — so on paper or white, use gold only as a thin accent (rules, edges, small marks) and reach for Bronze when gold text needs to stay legible. Never fill large light areas with gold.
